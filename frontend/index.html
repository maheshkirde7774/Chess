FOUR-PLAYER CHESS RULES

1. The 14×14 Cross Arena

Played on a 14×14 board with the 4 corner 3×3 areas removed (160 active squares). Armies start on the 4 banks: Cyan (South), Magenta (West), Lime (North), Gold (East).

2. Turn Order & Progression

Turns proceed clockwise: Cyan → Magenta → Lime → Gold → Cyan. When an eliminated player's turn comes, it is automatically bypassed.

3. Pawn Mechanics & 8th Rank Promotion

Pawns move forward into the arena (2 squares on their first move) and capture 1 square diagonally in their forward direction. Pawns promote to a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight upon reaching the 8th rank relative to their army or opposite perimeter.

4. Check, Checkmate & King Elimination

If an active enemy piece attacks your King, you are in Check! On your turn, you must make a move to resolve check. If no legal escape exists, you are Checkmated and eliminated.

5. Dormant Neutral Pieces

When a player is eliminated or resigns, their King is removed, and their remaining army becomes neutral obstacles. They do not attack or move, but can be captured.

6. Last Commander Standing

The last surviving player with their King intact wins the match!
