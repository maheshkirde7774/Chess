OFFICIAL FOUR-PLAYER CHESS RULES

1. The 14×14 Cross Arena

Played on a 14×14 board with the four 3×3 corner areas removed (160 active squares). Armies deploy on the four banks: Cyan (South), Magenta (West), Lime (North), and Gold (East).

2. Turn Progression

Turns proceed clockwise: Cyan → Magenta → Lime → Gold. Eliminated players are seamlessly bypassed.

3. 8th-Rank Promotion

Pawns march forward into the central theater (2 squares on their first move) and capture 1 square diagonally. Upon reaching the 8th rank relative to their army or the opposite perimeter, pawns promote to Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.

4. Check & Elimination

If an enemy piece attacks your King, you are in Check! If no legal evasion exists on your turn, you are eliminated.

5. Dormant Neutral Pieces

When an army is eliminated, its King is removed, and remaining soldiers become neutral obstacles. They cannot move or attack, but may be captured for positional clearance.

6. Last Commander Standing

The last player retaining an active King wins the tournament match!
